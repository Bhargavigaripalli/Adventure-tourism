Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your expedition.

What is your weather cancellation policy?

If extreme river swells or high mountain winds affect safety, your expedition is rescheduled at zero charge or converted into a 100% full refund voucher valid for 2 years.

Are safety gear and insurance included in the price?

Yes! All Petzl climbing harnesses, NRS life jackets, helmets, and $50,000 emergency medical rescue insurance are fully included in every package price.

What fitness level is required?

Day thrill passes and beach camping require moderate physical health. Summit trekking requires basic cardio stamina, and beginner options are available for all skill levels.
