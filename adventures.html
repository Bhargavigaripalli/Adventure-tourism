Expedition FAQs & Tips

Essential information to help you prepare before launching your next adventure.

What gear and clothing should I bring?

For river rafting, bring synthetic quick-dry clothing and extra footwear. For summit treks, bring warm thermal layers and sturdy ankle-high hiking boots. All technical safety gear (helmets, harnesses, life jackets, tents) is provided by Stackly Basecamp.

Are beginner thrill-seekers eligible for all activities?

Absolutely! All our trips cater to both first-timers and experienced adventurers. Every group is escorted by 1:2 guide ratios who conduct comprehensive safety briefings before launch.

What is the weather guarantee and cancellation policy?

If rain or high river swells prevent safe operation, you can reschedule for free or receive a 100% full-value voucher valid for 24 months.

How are photos and video recordings delivered?

Immediately following your expedition, your 4K GoPro media pack is uploaded to your private Stackly Club cloud portal, accessible via high-speed download links.
