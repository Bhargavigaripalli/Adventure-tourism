Our Origin Story

Built By Mountaineers, Driven By Passion

Founded in 2018 by veteran alpine expedition leader Vikram Rawat and wilderness survival instructor Maya Chen, Stackly Tourism was born out of a desire to redefine adventure travel in South Asia.

What started as a single white-water raft equipment rental hub in Rishikesh has grown into an internationally acclaimed adventure operator with over 18 certified guides, satellite safety dispatch hubs, and thousands of successful summit ascents.
